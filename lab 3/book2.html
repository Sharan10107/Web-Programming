Atomic Habits is a comprehensive, practical guide on how to change your habits and get 1% better every day. Using concepts from biology, psychology, and neuroscience, James Clear creates an easy-to-understand framework for making good habits inevitable and bad habits impossible.

Clear introduces the Four Laws of Behavior Change: Make it Obvious, Make it Attractive, Make it Easy, and Make it Satisfying. Rather than relying on willpower, you learn how small incremental changes accumulate into life-altering transformations.
