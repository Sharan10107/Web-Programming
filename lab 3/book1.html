The Alchemist is a worldwide philosophical phenomenon that tells the enchanting story of Santiago, an Andalusian shepherd boy who yearns to travel in search of a worldly treasure as extravagant as any ever found.

From his home in Spain he journeys into the markets of Tangier and across the Egyptian desert to a fateful encounter with the alchemist. The story teaches us about the essential wisdom of listening to our hearts, recognizing opportunity and learning to read the omens strewn along life's path, and, above all, following our dreams.
